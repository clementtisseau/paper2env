# Results

45 runs, three models (Claude Haiku 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, Opus 5.5 as Claude Code
subagents), levels L4 to L1, three runs per cell in most cases, all with the
`exp` starter (CPU training available) and a stated 2-hour budget. Every run
was re-graded with the final theory check. Full tables, including every run:
[`results_tables.md`](results_tables.md) (generated by
[`scripts/summarize_runs.py`](../scripts/summarize_runs.py)).

"Drift-free" below means the submitted loss passes the theory check: its
expected update has no drift at any prefix, under a mild and a severe
mismatch, is unchanged on-policy, and has bounded per-sample gradients. All
28 drift-free submissions use the sampler's next-token distribution and
describe centering or subtracting the score, i.e. the paper's method or a
variant of it; the reports were read to confirm this.

## What stands out

**Opus rediscovers the method from L1 in every run.** Given only the problem
and what was known before the paper, all five of its L1 runs are drift-free,
also with the top-k only. The reports derive the per-prefix drift
E_q[A|h]·(q − p) at the logits, discuss why group centering does not remove
it (it zeroes the advantage over whole sequences, not E[A|h] at each prefix),
center the score under the sampler and model the top-k tail; four of the
five also fold importance weights into the centering measure (q·w), as the
paper does. See
[`20261118-223834-exp-L1`](../runs/20261118-223834-exp-L1/REPORT.md).

**Weaker models get the diagnosis right and reach for the familiar remedy.**
Many failing Haiku and Sonnet reports at L1–L2 correctly show that
E_q[∇log p] = q − p ≠ 0 turns every baseline into a drift, then fix it with
importance sampling: unclipped token weights (unbiased but unbounded),
weights capped at a loose bound (exact until a ratio exceeds the cap), or
sequence-level weights. This is the near-miss the rubric lists as N5, and the
theory check's `bounded` and severe-mismatch checks exist to catch it.

**More hints are not always better.** Sonnet passes 3/3 at L3 but 0/3 at
L2a and L2b, below its L1 score (2/6). The L2 hints ask to analyze the
expected update under q, which seems to steer it toward reweighting the
expectation back to p, i.e. importance sampling. Sonnet also stops early
(median 5 commands and 2 minutes, against about 20 commands for the others),
so it rarely tests an idea before submitting. With three runs per cell this
is a pattern to confirm, not a measurement.

**The top-k is the hard part.** An inference engine returns only its top-k
logprobs. Truncating the sum to the head looks natural and is biased even
on-policy (Σ_head p ∇log p ≠ 0). Haiku is drift-free with the full
distribution in 9 runs but with the top-k in only 3; the tail model (q's tail
proportional to p, so the tail's term vanishes) is what separates a complete
answer from a partial one.

**Composition with importance sampling is mostly an Opus result.** Centering
under q while the sampled token carries a weight w(p/q) over-corrects as much
as REINFORCE drifts. Only Opus consistently centers under q·w.

**Framing.** 17 of the 28 drift-free reports call the correction a "control
variate". The paper insists it is a bias correction (on-policy it is exactly
zero; it is not there to reduce variance). The LLM judge's rubric caps that
framing, which is one reason a judge is needed on top of the theory check.

## The graders disagree where they should

- The CPU guard-rail passed 10 of the 17 submissions the theory check
  rejects. Expected: on a CPU-sized problem, importance-sampling variants
  stabilize training as well as the paper's method (see
  [lessons](lessons.md)). The guard-rail only answers "does it break
  training?".
- No submission passed the theory check and failed the guard-rail.

## Starter versions

The second starter (`v2`, 8 L1 runs) removed the setting that exposed the
sampler's distribution and always puts its top-20 in the loss batch, as an
inference engine would. Results at L1 are similar (Opus 2/2, Haiku 1/3,
Sonnet 0/3 against 3/3, 0/3 and 2/3 with `v1`), within the noise of three
runs.

## Caveats

Small samples, one harness, no enforced budget, no LLM judge yet: see
[lessons and limitations](lessons.md).
